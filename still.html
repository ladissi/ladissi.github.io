journey of meditation levi adissi

a cluttered mind.

what feels like endless thoughts. some pervasive.

scroll

one breath.

in for 4s, hold for 4s, out for 4s.

inholdout

meditation is the gym. come back to the present, every return contributes to your practice.

the droning settles.

a fit nervous system lowers stress faster. tap the line to lower reactivity.

you are not your thoughts.

you are the one watching them. let them go and return to the present.

one breath at a time.

thank you for joining me on this journey.
