email leviadissi@gmail.com and a person will answer. include your iOS version if the question is about a bug.

does phoneZero block apps?

no, and it does not pretend to. blocker apps exist, but a block you can switch off is a speed bump, not a wall. phoneZero makes a different bet: it removes the pull instead. your apps become a quiet list of words, with no grid, no icons, no badges. if you want to open something, you will open it.

how do i set up the home screen widgets?

long-press your home screen, tap the plus button, search for phoneZero, and add Widget 1. add Widget 2 to the same Smart Stack if you want up to twelve apps in one swipeable carousel. the editor inside the app shows exactly which apps land on which widget.

why does Camera need a shortcut?

iOS gives apps no public way to open the Camera directly. phoneZero walks you through creating a one-time Shortcut named Open Camera. it takes about thirty seconds and the app shows you every step.

how do i make phone calls?

use the built-in dialer: a real keypad with named favorites. type or tap, and iOS places the call. this exists because iOS also gives apps no way to open the Phone app directly.

an app will not open from the list

phoneZero opens apps by their public URL scheme. if an app is not installed, or its developer changed its scheme, the tap fails quietly. re-add the app from the built-in catalog, or use the custom app editor, which previews and tests the link before you save it.
