phoneZero privacy policy

effective august 13, 2026

the whole policy in one sentence

phoneZero collects nothing, sends nothing, and stores everything on your device.

what phoneZero collects

nothing. no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising identifiers, no account, no sign-in. the app contains no third-party SDKs and makes no network requests. it works in airplane mode because there is nothing to phone home to.

where your data lives

your app list, theme choice, dialer favorites, and any custom names you set are stored on your device in the app's own storage. they never leave it. deleting the app deletes them.

calls

the built-in dialer hands the number you typed to iOS, which places the call and shows its own confirmation. phoneZero keeps no call history and cannot see your calls. favorites you add are stored on your device like everything else.

changes

if a future version ever changes any of the above, this page will say so plainly before that version ships, and the App Store privacy label will be updated to match.
